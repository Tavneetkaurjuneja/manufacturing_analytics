# Manufacturing Analytics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing manufacturing operations data to evaluate production performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and uncover quality and efficiency trends.

The project was developed as a stakeholder reporting solution using SQL, Microsoft Excel, Tableau, and Power BI. The analysis focuses on production, rejection, wastage, department-wise performance, employee-wise performance, machine performance, and buyer trends.

## Objectives

- Analyse and monitor overall manufacturing performance.
- Identify bottlenecks across production and rejection processes.
- Evaluate department-wise, employee-wise, and machine-wise performance.
- Analyse production, rejection, and wastage trends.
- Identify key buyers contributing to overall demand.
- Present actionable insights through data visualisation.
- Support data-driven decision-making for stakeholders.

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL
- Microsoft Excel
- Tableau
- Power BI

## Key Insights

### Manufacturing Performance

- 49.8M units were manufactured during the analysed period.
- Monthly production remained relatively stable, ranging between 3.7M and 4.7M units.
- April recorded the highest production, while February recorded the lowest.

### Quality & Production Losses

- Approximately 491K units were rejected during production.
- Approximately 490K units were recorded as wastage.
- Combined rejection and wastage resulted in nearly 1M units of production loss.
- Knitwear and Printed Fabric showed comparatively higher rejection rates.

### Operational Bottlenecks

- A small number of employees and machines contributed significantly to overall rejections.
- Employee-wise and machine-wise analysis helped identify potential contributors to production inefficiencies.

### Buyer Analysis

- Uniqlo, Zara, H&M, and Nike emerged as major buyers.
- The analysis highlighted a significant concentration of purchases among key buyers.

## Production Trend Analysis

A monthly production trend analysis was performed using line charts to identify production fluctuations and seasonal patterns.

Key observations:

- **Steady Output:** Monthly production remained within a relatively consistent range of 3.7M–4.7M units.
- **April Peak:** April recorded the highest production at approximately 4.01M units.
- **February Dip:** February recorded the lowest production at approximately 3.71M units.
- **Stable Rejections:** Monthly rejections remained relatively consistent at around 40K units despite fluctuations in production.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate machines and employees associated with consistently high rejection volumes.
- Review processes within departments showing higher rejection rates, particularly Knitwear and Printed Fabric.
- Monitor rejection and wastage to identify opportunities for improving production efficiency.
- Track dependency on key buyers and explore opportunities for customer diversification.
- Continue monitoring monthly production and rejection trends to identify emerging operational patterns.

## Project Outcome

The project transformed raw manufacturing data into stakeholder-ready reports and dashboards, enabling users to monitor production performance, identify quality issues, evaluate operational bottlenecks, and support data-driven decision-making.
